SAVIOR: State-Guided Verified Rollouts for Long-Horizon Video Generation in Robotic Manipulation

Anonymous Authors

arXiv Video Code (coming soon)

Abstract

Recent advances in video generation have enabled scalable synthesis of robotic manipulation trajectories directly from task instructions. However, existing approaches remain unreliable for long-horizon manipulation because trajectory generation is performed through open-loop autoregressive rollout, where locally inconsistent transitions recursively corrupt future task evolution despite visually plausible intermediate motions. This limitation is particularly critical in robotics, where successful execution depends not only on visual realism, but on preserving executable interaction consistency across extended temporal horizons. We therefore reformulate robotic video generation as verified executable state evolution and propose SAVIOR: State-Guided Verified Rollouts for Long-Horizon Video Generation in Robotic Manipulation, a closed-loop framework that incrementally constructs manipulation trajectories through sequential planning, task-conditioned generation, transition verification, and persistent rollout tracking. Instead of directly propagating generated observations through autoregressive continuation, SAVIOR verifies each intermediate transition before rollout propagation, preventing invalid interactions and infeasible task progression from destabilizing downstream generation. Furthermore, SAVIOR performs verification at the level of manipulation state transitions rather than dense frame realism, aligning rollout evaluation with executable interaction consistency. Across diverse video generators and long-horizon manipulation tasks, SAVIOR consistently improves compositional task completion, interaction consistency, and rollout executability. Our analysis further shows that many long-horizon failures arise not from catastrophic generation collapse, but from the accumulation of locally inconsistent transitions during rollout evolution, highlighting the importance of verified state-guided rollout for reliable robotic video generation.

Architecture

Overview. SAVIOR frames robotic video generation as closed-loop task-state evolution. Each module is verified before its output affects the rollout, keeping long-horizon behavior stable.

Planner
